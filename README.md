DEKUT Medical Centre Management System

A web-based Medical Centre Management System developed for Dedan Kimathi University of Technology (DEKUT) to digitize and streamline healthcare operations. The system provides a centralized platform for managing patients, medical records, appointments, referrals, staff, and other administrative activities within the medical centre.

📌 About the Project

The DEKUT Medical Centre Management System was developed to address challenges associated with manual healthcare record management and administrative processes.

Traditional paper-based processes can result in:

Excessive paperwork
Difficulty retrieving patient records
Loss or duplication of information
Delays in managing appointments and referrals
Difficulty coordinating medical centre activities
Limited access to accurate and up-to-date information

This system provides a centralized digital platform that helps medical centre staff manage information more efficiently while improving accessibility, accuracy, and organization of records.
